## Design Principles

- **Single-Column Mobile Hierarchy:** Guaranteed readability on small viewport devices without horizontal overflow.
- **Scannable Content Cards:** Clear visual separation between featured stories, product launches, and editorial notes.
- **High-Contrast Call-to-Action (CTA):** Bulletproof button styling engineered to maximize click-through rates.
- **Typography & Dark Mode Compatibility:** Semantic contrast ratios ensuring clear legibility across dark and light inbox clients.
